FruntDesk

Booking, reminders, and no-show protection — without the front desk.

FruntDesk is a booking-management agent for solo, appointment-based operators — barbers, tattoo artists, mechanics, and similar single-person shops. It syncs your existing Google Calendar, texts your clients a reminder before their appointment, lets them confirm, reschedule, or cancel themselves, and can collect a deposit up front to protect your time.

Two-way calendar sync

Appointments booked through FruntDesk appear on your Google Calendar, and changes you make there sync back — no double-booking.

Automatic reminders

Clients get a text before their appointment with a link to confirm, reschedule, or cancel — no back-and-forth calls.

Deposits & no-show protection

Collect a deposit at booking. Show up, it's credited; no-show, it's kept — protecting your schedule.

Who it's for

FruntDesk is built for operators who run their business solo — one calendar, one set of hands, one person answering the phone. If that's you, FruntDesk handles the scheduling admin so you don't have to.
