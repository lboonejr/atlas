Researched the general NJ-CRC framework for cannabis consumption-area ("lounge")
endorsements (web search, 2026-09-18 — see Sources; not Newark-specific ordinance
text, which still needs local confirmation):

- A cannabis consumption area endorsement is added to an existing Class 5
  retailer/dispensary license — it is not a separate business license.
- **Municipal support is a hard NJ-CRC gate, not just a courtesy step**: the CRC will
  automatically reject a consumption-area endorsement application that lacks explicit
  municipal approval. Applicants need a **formal resolution from the municipal
  governing body** (i.e., Newark's Municipal Council, not just one councilman's
  personal signature) specifically supporting the consumption area at that location.
- Municipalities can also adopt their own local ordinance on consumption areas
  (e.g., capping how many are allowed citywide, imposing extra conditions) — Newark's
  specific ordinance, if any, and which city department/committee actually processes
  the resolution request, are not confirmed by this research and need a direct check
  (Newark's cannabis licensing office or the City Clerk's office would be the
  starting point).
- Beyond municipal endorsement, the CRC's own facility/safety/ventilation
  requirements for consumption areas apply once the municipal piece is secured.

**Read on "the councilman didn't sign it":** this matches the pattern above — a single
council member's individual sign-off is very likely not what the CRC actually needs;
what's needed is a governing-body **resolution**, which usually requires the matter to
be introduced and voted on at a public council meeting, not just one member's
signature on a letter. If that's what happened here (Marconi sent something for one
councilman's personal signature rather than getting it on a council meeting agenda for
a vote), that could explain the stall — but this is a read, not confirmed fact; worth
Lemar/Marconi checking directly with Newark's council office on the actual resolution
process before pushing the same paper again.
