# Newark facade grant — see if The Station qualifies

Lemar wants to find out whether there's a grant program that helps Newark businesses
cover the cost of fixing up their building facades, with The Station (Newark) in mind
as the business that could use it.

  county, or a nonprofit/BID), and what it covers.

## Update 2026-09-06 — research findings (Convo 1 card, Option A)

Two candidate programs, web research only — nothing applied for or confirmed with an
agency:

1. **Invest Newark — UEZ Façade Improvement Grant Program.** Run by Invest Newark
   (the city's Urban Enterprise Zone administrator), currently a pilot limited to the
   **West Ward and South Ward** (a separate South Ward application PDF is public), with
   other Newark neighborhoods to follow "soon" — unclear if that's happened yet.
   Requires a detailed budget + three licensed-contractor estimates, proof of fire/
   extended-coverage and flood insurance, and (if leased) the landlord's signed
   permission. A "deadline to apply: March 31, 2026" appears in search results — that
   date has already passed as of today (9/6/2026), so this may be describing a closed
   cycle; whether a new cycle is open needs a direct check with Invest Newark
   (investnewark.org), not assumed from search results.
2. **NJEDA Small Business Improvement Grant** — statewide, not Newark-specific: up to
   $50,000 reimbursement for building improvements (facade included) or new furniture/
   fixtures/equipment; 40% of funding reserved for Opportunity Zone census tracts.
   (njeda.gov/small-business-improvement-grant)

**Open questions before applying:**
- Which Newark ward/neighborhood The Station sits in — determines UEZ pilot
  eligibility (West Ward / South Ward only, as far as this research shows).
- Whether the UEZ facade program has a currently-open cycle (the 3/31/2026 date found
  may be stale/closed).
- Whether The Station's address falls in an Opportunity Zone tract for the NJEDA
  program's set-aside.
- Still unconfirmed against the other two open Convo 2 threads ("Newark Grant
  Application" / "Newark Renewal Application", both 8/31) — not yet checked whether
  those are the same program.

Nothing filed or applied for. Recommend a direct call/email to Invest Newark to confirm
current cycle status before spending more time on this.
