# AI-onboarding intake form — Stormy bake

Seed idea from Lemar, dropped via #decisions ("I'm ready, let's get it moving") and
carried into #stormy for the 15-question pressure test: an **AI-onboarding intake
form** — a Jotform basic-info / tools-in-use / pain-points / desired-outcome intake
that feeds straight into Samira's build loop whenever a new person or business comes
on board (dad, a booking-agent friend, etc). No deadline attached — Stormy territory,
not Shortlist.

This note backfills the pressure-test record that should have landed when the bake
started (PART Q ran two scans without a note existing — corrected here so the bake is
resumable from the note per the stormy-ideation loop's "note is state" rule). Nothing
below is new; it consolidates what already happened in #stormy.

## Pressure test — Section A/B (in progress)

**Q1 — What's the core problem or opportunity this solves?**
Lemar's answer (verbatim): "The core problem is that business operators (or
individuals) often have issues taking care of some of the day to day elements of their
jobs or businesses. Appointment or booking based business often struggle managing said
bookings. Lead based jobs struggle with finding leads. Businesses have trouble managing
inventory. Individuals are always looking to improve their workflows. I think the
solution comes in identifying this problem and that's what the jotform will serve as,
the front door on identifying the person (or business, and the problem)."
Read as: appointment/booking chaos, lead-gen struggles, inventory headaches, general
"I want this to run smoother" — Other, with Jotform as the front-door diagnostic.

**Q2 — Primary beneficiary? (open, asked, no answer yet)**
Staying internal for now (Lemar, family, close network — dad, the booking-agent
friend) or already picturing this as a productized offering for External
partner/Customers down the line?

**Q3 — Scope + hardest constraint? (open, asked, no answer yet)**
First version — just the intake form itself, or the form plus Lemar personally
building the automation after someone fills it out? Biggest likely slow-down: his own
time to build each intake, Jotform/connector limits, or something else?

## Pressure test plan (migrated 2026-09-05)

Size: **small-for-now** — one owner (Lemar), one surface (Jotform → Samira's build
loop), nothing external moves, cheap to undo. Flagging that dimension 2's answer could
bump this to medium if the beneficiary answer comes back "external/productized." 4-7
questions target; 3 new asks this round (all crux) plus the one already answered.

1. **Problem & payoff — ASSUME** (from the answered old Q1, verbatim in this note above):
   business operators/individuals struggle with day-to-day operational elements —
   bookings, lead-gen, inventory, general workflow — and the Jotform serves as the front
   door on identifying who's asking and what's actually broken.
2. **Scope & hardest constraint — ASK, crux** (was old Q2+Q3, unanswered): staying
   internal (Lemar, family, close network — Dad, the booking-agent friend) or picturing
   this as a real productized External offering down the line? And for v1: just the
   intake form, or the form plus Lemar personally building the automation after someone
   fills it out?
3. **Success & failure — ASK, crux** (new, not asked under the old form): what makes a
   completed intake actually useful — the smallest signal it worked? And the tell it
   isn't (nobody fills it out, the answers don't lead anywhere actionable, it takes more
   of his time than it saves)?
4. **Dependencies & risk — ASSUME**: nothing new has to exist — the Jotform connector and
   Samira's build loop both already run; the main risk is Lemar's own time to build each
   intake's automation, or a Jotform/connector limit.
5. **Timing & preconditions — ASK, crux**: does this wait for a live candidate already
   lined up (Dad, the booking-agent friend), or ship standalone before anyone specific is
   queued to use it?
6. **Blast radius & reversibility — ASSUME**: personal-owned Jotform, nothing moves money
   or leaves Lemar's control while it stays internal (per dimension 2's default read) —
   easy to edit or kill. Reassess if dimension 2 comes back External.
7. **Automation & data flow — ASSUME**: a Jotform submission feeds Samira's build loop /
   Haven capture, same pattern as any other intake; no new source of truth.
8. **Ownership & upkeep — ASSUME**: Lemar owns it; it runs through Samira's existing
   build loop, no new agent or skill to maintain.

Asks: 3 (all crux — 2, 3, 5). Assumed: 5 dimensions. N/A: none.
