# P00 Client intake system

## What this is

The engagement's front door. One master Jotform that collects everything we need from the
client group to position them and build their application, plus a written register that
turns every blank answer into an open item with an owner, a phase, a lane, and a route to
producing it.

Built because the engagement had no structured way to get the group's facts. Everything we
did not know lived in a chat thread or in somebody's head, and the Working Log carried
three unknowns (the group's name, the property address, the contact) that nothing was
actually chasing.

Form: Camden Dispensary Launch — Client Intake, https://form.jotform.com/262300294666053
Jotform asset id 262300294666053. Eleven sections, save and resume on, uploads throughout.

Sections: who is filling it out · the entity · the people · designation and license path ·
the property · local approvals · state filing status · advisors and vendors ·
communications already in flight · capital and timing · anything else.

Two build choices worth recording.

One master form rather than a master plus a per-principal form. One thing to send, one
thing to chase. The cost is that a single contact answers the owner-level questions on
behalf of everyone in round one, and we chase per-person detail in Phase 00 conversation.

No identifiers anywhere. No Social Security numbers, no tax identification numbers, no
bank account numbers, no dates of birth. Where the Commission will eventually want an
identifier, the form asks only whether the document exists and is ready. The background
question is a yes or no with an instruction not to describe it, because that conversation
belongs with their attorney.

## Build lane

Mixed, split three ways.

CLOUD, done in this thread: the form itself through the Jotform connector, the register,
this note, the Drive copies, the project knowledge base record, the card, and the reports
line. Everything downstream of a submission is also cloud: reading it, writing the gap
list, drafting the request emails as drafts, and setting reminder events on dated items.

BROWSER, needs Lemar present: wiring the Jotform to Google Drive integration (behind his
Jotform login), the entity and property record lookups, the City clerk and permit record
checks, and anything on the Commission licensing portal. Three run blocks are in the
register. All of them read, fill, and capture. None of them submit, pay, or click a
binding button.

LOCAL: the documents themselves. The executed lease, the operating agreement, the
architect's drawings, the resolution. We request those, we do not produce them, and the
entity and capital documents are their counsel's and their accountant's work, not ours.

Six gates, run against the build.

Scope gate, clear. Every section maps to something an inspector, the City, or the
Commission checks. The vendor page asks who the point of sale vendor is, which is seed to
sale tracking and inside scope, and does not ask about menu, pricing, or margin.

Authority gate, clear with one deliberate route. The background disclosure question is a
yes or no that explicitly routes the conversation to their attorney. The capital page asks
what is committed and where it comes from and gives no advice on structure, tax, or the
raise.

Outcome gate, clear. Nothing on the form or the thank you page implies a license or an
inspection result. The thank you page says we come back with open items, not with an
approval.

Fact gate, clear. The form asks and does not assume. It carries no date, dollar amount, or
requirement that we invented. The register's browser runs are written to read published
criteria rather than state them from memory.

Approvals gate, clear and this is the one that mattered. Page 6 asks the planning board
site plan approval and the City of Camden resolution of local support as separate
questions from separate bodies, with an intro that says so in plain words, and each has
its own upload.

Placement gate, clear with one call made. The full register names our lanes and how we
chase the group, so it goes to 02 Internal. A short client-facing intake instruction sheet
carrying the form link goes to 01 Client-Facing, Phase 00, Intake. Nothing is superseded.

failures on the same sub-task, so it stops here and moves to the browser lane rather than
taking a third attempt at it.

One thing worth knowing for anyone who runs this connector again: a fetch taken while a
Jotform edit is still applying reports a partial form. Mid-edit this one read 91 questions
against its true 121. Do not read a mid-edit fetch as data loss and do not act on one.
Re-fetch until the count settles.
