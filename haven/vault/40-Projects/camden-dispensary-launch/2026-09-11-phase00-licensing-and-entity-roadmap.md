# Camden Launch — Phase 00 licensing readiness + business-legitimization roadmap (internal working draft)

letter is on file yet (Working Log last entry 8/24; per the 2026-09-11 index update,

## Track A — CRC licensing readiness (Phase 00 → 01)

Pulled from the Working Log's own Phase 00 tracker (index.md, all currently unchecked):

1. □ Planning board approval read — conditions + expiration recorded
2. □ Site control confirmed or flagged missing
3. □ Entity / cap table / operating agreement reviewed or formed
4. □ Designation strategy set (Camden impact-zone status)
5. □ Capital requirement sized start-to-finish
6. □ Position Report delivered

Plus the Phase 01 gate items (Site Control and Local Endorsement, per the overlay):
7. □ City of Camden resolution of local support obtained (separate from, and in
   addition to, the planning board site plan approval already held — the overlay
   flags these as commonly confused)
8. □ Site control instrument (lease/purchase) executed, not just negotiated

**Genuinely unknown, not to be guessed** (per the index's own "Unknown" section):
the group's entity name, property address, and contact; the planning board
approval's conditions and expiration; whether site control is executed. Items 1, 2,
7, and 8 above can't move until the group supplies these.

## Track B — business legitimization / entity formation

General process orientation only — this is not legal, tax, or accounting advice
(overlay authority gate); an attorney/accountant should handle actual filings. NJ
process, for whatever entity form the group chooses (commonly an LLC for a licensed
cannabis operator):

1. □ Choose and reserve the entity name (NJ Business Name Search, njportal.com)
2. □ File the Certificate of Formation with NJ Division of Revenue & Enterprise
   Services (DORES) — creates the legal entity
3. □ Draft and execute an Operating Agreement (internal document; not filed with the
   state, but the CRC application will ask for it — ties to Track A item 3)
4. □ Obtain a federal EIN from the IRS (free, directly via irs.gov — no third-party
   filing fee needed; commonly same-day online for a US-based responsible party)
5. □ Register for NJ state taxes (NJ-REG, via the Division of Revenue) — needed
   before the entity can legally do business or hold a cannabis license in NJ
6. □ Open a business bank account (needs the EIN + formation documents + operating
   agreement — cannabis banking may require a cannabis-friendly institution)
7. □ Confirm NJ good standing once formed — the same Business Name Search used for
   Track A item 7 above also reports standing on a newly formed entity

**Where this connects to Cuzzie's own standing issue:** Cuzzie's Dispensary &
Delivery LLC (Lemar's own entity, unrelated to the Camden Group) currently has an
open NJ annual-report revocation notice
([[2026-08-17-nj-annual-report-revocation-notice]]) — a live example of what
Track B item 7 is checking for, and a reminder to keep the Camden Group's own
annual-report filings current once formed (a common way entities lapse into
revocation risk years later).

## What Samira can and can't do here

Can: hold this checklist, cross-reference the Working Log, flag gaps, draft the
01 client-facing version once the letter is signed. Cannot: file anything with the
state or IRS on the group's behalf, give binding legal/tax advice, or promise any
outcome or date to the group. The EIN application (Track B, item 4) in particular is
something only the entity's own responsible party can file (IRS requires the
applicant's own SSN/ITIN or existing EIN).
