## What NJ has (informational only, not a benefits application)

Two main state-run paid-family-caregiver routes:

- **Personal Preference Program (PPP)** — for NJ FamilyCare/Medicaid beneficiaries who
  need personal-care assistance. Participant gets a monthly self-directed budget and
  can hire the caregiver of their choice, including a relative (does not bar a spouse
  either). Requires Ella to already be on NJ FamilyCare/Medicaid with a personal-care
  need determination.
- **Jersey Assistance for Community Caregiving (JACC)** — state-funded, for seniors
  60+ who need nursing-facility-level care but want to stay home, and who are **not**
  on Medicaid/NJ FamilyCare or MLTSS. Lets a qualifying senior hire an eligible family
  member, friend, or neighbor as a paid caregiver. Income cap ~365% of the federal
  poverty level (2026: ~$4,855/mo individual, ~$6,582/mo couple). Service cap ~$1,090/
  month plus care management, with an income-based sliding-scale copay. Covers care
  management, personal care, respite, home modifications, medical equipment, chore
  services, a personal emergency response system, home-delivered meals, adult day
  care, and transportation.

Which one fits depends on whether Ella is already enrolled in Medicaid/NJ FamilyCare
(→ PPP) or not (→ JACC likely, if under the income cap). Neither is applied for here —
this is orientation only.
