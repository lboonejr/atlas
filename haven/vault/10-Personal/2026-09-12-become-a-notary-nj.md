## General NJ notary process (orientation only, not legal advice)

- Eligibility: 18+, NJ resident or work primarily in NJ, able to read/write English,
  no disqualifying criminal record.
- Get a legislative sponsor: a NJ notary application must be endorsed/sponsored by a
  member of the NJ Legislature (State Senator or Assembly member) representing the
  applicant's district — this is NJ's distinctive requirement vs. most other states.
- Complete the application (Division of Revenue and Enterprise Services, NJ Treasury)
  and pay the filing fee (a nominal state fee, historically in the $15-25 range —
  worth confirming the current figure directly at nj.gov before relying on it).
- No exam or training course is currently mandated by NJ for a first-time notary
  commission (unlike some states) — confirm this hasn't changed.
- Take the oath of office once commissioned, then obtain a notary seal/stamp.
- Commission term is 5 years in NJ; renewal follows a similar process.
