# Mom's carpet — vomit cleanup plan

Lemar dropped this in Convo 2 (self-DM, ts `1788714808.261809`, 13:13 ET 2026-09-06):
his mom's short-pile carpet needs cleaning after she vomited on it, and he needs to
know what tools/equipment to use, tomorrow.

## What to use
- **Enzyme-based cleaner** (e.g. Nature's Miracle, or a DIY mix of equal parts white
  vinegar + water + a small squirt of dish soap) — enzymes break down the organic
  matter that causes the smell, plain soap/water alone won't fully kill it.
- **Wet/shop vac** if available (best for a short-pile carpet), otherwise old towels
  + a stiff scrub brush.
- **Rubber gloves.**
- **Baking soda** for the overnight odor pull.

## Steps
1. Blot up all solid/liquid residue first (scrape/blot, don't rub it in).
2. Apply the enzyme cleaner, let it sit per the product's instructions (usually
   10-15 min).
3. Blot dry (or wet-vac it up).
4. Sprinkle baking soda generously over the spot, let sit overnight.
5. Vacuum the baking soda up in the morning.
