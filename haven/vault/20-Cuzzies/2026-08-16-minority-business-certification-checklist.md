## Which certification actually matters here

There are two different things that both get called "MBE certification," and
for a NJ cannabis business the state one is what counts:

1. **NJ Division of Revenue & Enterprise Services (DORES) — Minority and/or
   Women Business Enterprise (M/WBE) certification.** This is the one the NJ
   Cannabis Regulatory Commission (CRC) actually recognizes: CRC's "Diversely
   Owned Business" priority-application category requires certification
   **issued by the NJ Department of the Treasury** specifically. This is the
   relevant path for Cuzzie's.
2. **NMSDC (National Minority Supplier Development Council) certification.**
   A private national supplier-diversity certification used mostly for
   corporate supply-chain/procurement purposes. Not required for CRC
   priority status; only worth pursuing if Cuzzie's wants to sell into
   corporate/vendor supply chains that specifically ask for NMSDC status.

## Path 1 — NJ Treasury M/WBE certification (the one that matters for CRC)

- **Certifying body:** NJ Division of Revenue and Enterprise Services (DORES),
  Small Business Registration & M/WBE Certification Services unit —
  (609) 292-2146.
- **Where to apply:** streamlined web-based application at
  business.nj.gov (certifications page) — one application can cover
  multiple categories at once (M/WBE, Small Business Enterprise (SBE),
  Disabled Veteran-Owned (DVOB), Socially and Economically Disadvantaged
  (SEDB)).
- **Fee:** the normal $100 application fee is currently **waived
  indefinitely** for Small/Minority/Women/Veteran/Disabled
  Veteran/Socially-Economically-Disadvantaged/LGBTQ+-owned business
  categories.
- **Documents typically required:** ownership documentation (operating
  agreement/cap table showing 51%+ minority ownership), organizational
  records (formation docs, EIN), personal net worth statement(s) for the
  qualifying owner(s), and evidence of operational control (who actually
  runs day-to-day operations).
- **Timeline:** not stated precisely in public sources for this specific
  program — worth calling the unit directly (number above) for a current
  estimate once Lemar is ready to apply.
- **Relevance to Cuzzie's:** CRC's "Diversely Owned Business" priority
  category (one of three priority tracks, alongside Social Equity and
  Impact Zone businesses) requires this exact Treasury certification.
  Whether it still matters for Cuzzie's given the current wind-down/sale
  context is Lemar's call — flagging as a live option, not assuming intent.

## Path 2 — NMSDC certification (only if a supply-chain use case exists)

- **Certifying body:** National Minority Supplier Development Council
  (nmsdc.org).
- **Requirements:** business must be 51%+ owned, operated, and controlled by
  one or more members of a recognized minority group (Asian-Indian,
  Asian-Pacific, Black, Hispanic, or Native American), and a U.S. citizen.
- **Process:** standards-based review — document review, interviews, and
  potentially a site visit to verify ownership/control/day-to-day authority.
- **Fee:** $270–$1,700/year depending on business revenue (under $1M revenue
  is at the low end).
- **Timeline:** NMSDC targets 45 business days; real-world turnaround
  commonly runs 60–90 days from a complete application.
- **Renewal:** annual; renewal application due within 90 days of expiration.
