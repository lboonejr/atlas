## Why this is an open item and not just a logged fact

The agreement is now in writing, and three material terms are undefined. None of them can
be resolved by reading the thread, so none are assumed here.

**1. Is the 10% additive or shared?** Peter's phrasing ("10% commission same as your
current broker") reads most naturally as *he receives 10%, at the same rate the existing
broker receives* — that is, a second 10%, not a split of one. But it can also be read as
"I step in at the same terms." On a license/business sale these are very different
numbers, and Lemar's "Yes, that is correct" answers the sentence without disambiguating
which reading he agreed to.

**2. There is an existing broker.** Introducing a second broker into the same buyer pool
is where procuring-cause disputes come from. Nothing in the thread carves out which buyers
belong to whom, and Haason's team was introduced through Peter.

**3. No scope, no trigger, no document.** Not stated: what the 10% is a percentage OF
(gross sale price, net proceeds, license value), what event triggers it, whether it
survives if the deal closes with a buyer Peter did not introduce, or whether the existing
broker's agreement permits a co-broke at all. There is no signed agreement, only two
emails.

**No figure is asserted here.** The sale price is not stated in this thread, so the dollar
exposure of 10% (or 20%) is unknown and deliberately left uncomputed.
