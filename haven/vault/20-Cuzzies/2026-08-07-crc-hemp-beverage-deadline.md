# CRC — intoxicating hemp beverage grace period ends Nov 13, 2026

General compliance advisory from the NJ Cannabis Regulatory Commission (Marie-Alaina
Murdza, Assistant Director, Office of Compliance and Investigations), received
2026-08-07 ~15:56 ET (Gmail thread `19fddcdc2c3b4457`) — sent broadly, not
Cuzzie's-specific (To: field is the sender's own address).

Intoxicating hemp-derived products, including intoxicating hemp beverages, may be
carried and sold by licensees through **November 13, 2026** provided they meet THC,
testing, and labeling requirements — untracked through METRC during this grace
period. Carrying such products past that date triggers enforcement action.

No reply needed (informational). Logged as a `due` note so it surfaces on the
reminder calendar and can be checked against Cuzzie's/Station inventory ahead of the
deadline — action is confirming (with whoever handles inventory) that no intoxicating
hemp beverages are on shelves past 11/13, not a task for Samira to execute.
