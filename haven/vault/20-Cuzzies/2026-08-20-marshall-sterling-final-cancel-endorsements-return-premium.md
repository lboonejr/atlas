## The open question — where does the $9,189.00 actually go?

Neither email names a payee, a premium finance company, a lienholder, or a loss payee.
Two readings are live and the difference is material:

1. **Already applied.** The cancellation date (6/5/2026) matches FIRST Insurance Funding
   loan **#106241219** exactly, and Tiffany Coleman confirmed on 2026-08-19 that
   **$10,907.40 unearned premium had already been applied** to that loan, leaving a
   balance of **$452.60** (past due $150.77) — see
   [[2026-07-23-first-insurance-funding-account-cancelled]], Update 2026-08-19. On this
   reading these endorsements are the carrier paperwork *behind* money already credited,
   and nothing new is coming.
2. **Additional.** $6,524.00 + $2,665.00 = **$9,189.00**, which does not equal the
   $10,907.40 already applied — a **$1,718.40 gap** in either direction depending on
   whether a third endorsement exists. If this $9,189.00 is *additional* return premium,
   it would first retire the $452.60 still owed on #106241219, and the remainder —
   roughly **$8,736** — would be refundable to Cuzzie's.

FIRST's own written position (Cory Smith, 2026-07-24, point 5) is that unearned premium
returned by the carrier "will be applied to reduce the balance due" — which governs only
up to the balance. It does not say what happens to a surplus once the loan is satisfied.

**Nothing is assumed here and no figure is treated as recoverable.** The amount that
reaches Cuzzie's, if any, is unconfirmed and stays unconfirmed until Marshall & Sterling
or FIRST puts it in writing. Raised as ONE #decisions parent with a drafted confirmation
email to Segler/Giampaolo — nothing sent, nothing paid, nothing claimed.

Note this is a *different* line from EPLI/Berkley Select loan **#105889646** (policy
DEP-2335743-P1, balance $10,466.68, past due $6,006.80), which Lemar decided on 7/6 to
let lapse — see [[2026-07-23-epli-berkley-select-cancellation-confirmed]]. Do not net the
two loans together.

## Why it matters beyond the ledger
Cuzzie's is winding down under broad collections pressure and the reopening plan in
[[index]] (`40-Projects/on-button-reopen`) is gated on cash. A confirmed refund in the
high four figures would be a live input to that plan; an assumed one would be a wrong
premise baked into it. Hence: confirm in writing first.
