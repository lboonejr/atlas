## Core definitions

- **Acknowledgment** — a declaration by an individual before a notarial officer that
  the individual has signed a record for the purpose stated in the record and, if
  signed in a representative capacity, that the individual signed with proper
  authority and as the act of the individual or entity identified in the record.
- **Verification on oath or affirmation** — a declaration, made by an individual on
  oath or affirmation before a notarial officer, that a statement in a record is true.
- **Notarial officer** — a notary public or other individual authorized by law to
  perform a notarial act. Every notary public is a notarial officer, but not every
  notarial officer is a notary public.
- **Notary public** — an individual commissioned by the State Treasurer to perform a
  notarial act.
- **Record** — information inscribed on a tangible medium, or stored in an electronic
  or other medium and retrievable in perceivable form. Deliberately broader than
  "paper document."
- **Sign** — with present intent to authenticate or adopt a record, to execute or
  adopt a tangible symbol, or to attach to or logically associate with the record an
  electronic symbol, sound, or process.
- **Official stamp** — a physical image affixed to or embossed on a tangible record,
  or an electronic image attached to or logically associated with an electronic record.
- **Stamping device** — the physical device that affixes or embosses the stamp, or the
  electronic device or process that attaches or logically associates it.
